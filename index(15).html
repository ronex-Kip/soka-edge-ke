Real Odds Analyser & Match Verdict

Pick a league and two teams. The tool pulls real recent results and head-to-head history from a public football feed, builds a Poisson goal model, then leans it with the same "soft" factors bookmakers' own traders use — squad news, motivation, media/tipster lean and derby history — before comparing it against the prices you see on Kenyan books (SportPesa, Betika, Odibets, Betway KE, 1xBet, 22Bet). It returns one clear call: who is likely to win, and whether the price is worth a KES stake after excise and withholding tax.

Why the model leans this way
Form and fixture data: TheSportsDB public feed. Odds are entered by you from Kenyan-licensed bookmakers. This is an analysis tool, not betting advice and not a betting service — the bookmaker's margin plus 15% excise and 20% withholding tax means most punters lose over time. Betting in Kenya is licensed by the BCLB and restricted to 18+. If it stops being fun, talk to someone — the Kenya Red Cross counselling line 1199 is free.
